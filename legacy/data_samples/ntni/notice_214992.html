OLMSTED LOCKS & DAM LOCK CHAMBER CLOSURE OHIO RIVER MILE 964.4

REFERENCE:

Navigation Notice No. 2026-042, dated August 11, 2026

 

Olmsted Locks and Dam (Ohio River Mile 964.4): In reference to Notice Number 214889 (Local Notice# 2026-042) Olmsted’s Land Chamber Closure: The USACE Medium Capacity Fleet will be onsite on 8 September 2026 and currently anticipates repairs to be completed by 21 September 2026.  Once the Land Chamber (1200’) repairs are completed the chamber will be re-opened for Navigation Traffic.  All traffic shall continue to utilize the River Chamber (1200’) until the repairs are completed. 

 

The River Chamber (1200’) will be closed from 5 October 2026 through 13 November 2026 for the USACE Heavy Capacity Fleet to perform a dewatering and make repairs to miter gates.  All traffic will use the Land Chamber (1200’) during this time.

 

Navigation Traffic Shall take all direction from the Olmsted Lock Tower.
